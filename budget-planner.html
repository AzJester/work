Cost

Cost by year

Cost by engine

Labor is the hours above multiplied by each engine's rate (the plan rate unless an engine has its own under Settings); materials are the dollars above. Committed is labor plus materials, and Remaining is the annual budget minus committed, marked Over when the plan exceeds it. Backlog cost is backlog hours at the rate plus backlog materials.

Past portions of each scheduled block count as worked or spent and future portions as forecast, pro-rated by day (or by weekday) across the dates the block spans; the as-of day itself is forecast. A block with hours logged or materials spent to date uses that figure and carries the rest of its estimate as forecast, so an overrun shows above the estimate instead of disappearing. Dated log entries count only up to the as-of date, so moving the as-of date back shows the plan as it stood then. A block that crosses a year boundary splits between years by day. Backlog items have no dates, so they sit outside the yearly totals. Annual hours and the annual budget are editable; leave one blank and its Remaining line reads Not set.

    Working the plan

    Click
    Open a block or backlog card for editing. Rows in the Blocks list open the same editor.
    Drag
    Move a block along the timeline. Drag either edge to change its start or end date.
    ← →
    Move the focused block by a day. Hold Shift to move it a week.

    Lengthen or shorten the focused block by a day at its end. Hold Shift for a week.
    L

    Delete
    Remove the focused block. Undo is in the toast.

    Undo the last change (Cmd on a Mac). The last 25 changes are kept.

    Engine name
    Click an engine's name on the timeline to see that engine alone in every section. Click it again, or the chip in the section bar, to show everything.
    As of
    Days before the as-of date count as worked or spent; the as-of day and everything after it count as forecast. Log entries dated after it are not counted yet.
    Logged
    Dated log entries are the record of a block's work. A block without entries can carry plain running totals instead.
    Rate
    Labor cost is hours at the plan rate in the Cost header, or at an engine's own rate from Settings. Materials are dollars as entered.
    Saving
    The plan saves in this browser as you work, with the last eight saves kept as backups under Settings. Export writes a JSON file you can import on another computer.
