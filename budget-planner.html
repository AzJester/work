Cost

Cost by year

Cost by engine

Labor is the hours above multiplied by the rate; materials are the dollars above. Committed is labor plus materials, and Remaining is the annual budget minus committed, marked Over when the plan exceeds it. Backlog cost is backlog hours at the rate plus backlog materials.

Past portions of each scheduled block count as worked or spent and future portions as forecast, pro-rated by day across the dates the block spans; the as-of day itself is forecast. A block with hours logged or materials spent to date uses that figure and carries the rest of its estimate as forecast, so an overrun shows above the estimate instead of disappearing. A block that crosses a year boundary splits between years by day. Backlog items have no dates, so they sit outside the yearly totals. Annual hours and the annual budget are editable; leave one blank and its Remaining line reads Not set.

    Working the plan

    Click
    Open a block or backlog card for editing. Rows in the Blocks list open the same editor.
    Drag
    Move a block along the timeline. Drag either edge to change its start or end date.
    ← →
    Move the focused block by a day. Hold Shift to move it a week.
    + -
    Lengthen or shorten the focused block by a day at its end. Hold Shift for a week.
    Delete
    Remove the focused block. Undo is in the toast.
    Ctrl Z
    Undo the last change (Cmd on a Mac). The last 25 changes are kept.
    N
    Add a block.
    B
    Add a backlog item.
    ?
    Show this help.
    As of
    Days before the as-of date count as worked or spent; the as-of day and everything after it count as forecast.
    Logged
    Enter hours logged to date and materials spent to date on a scheduled block and the plan uses those figures instead of the elapsed share of the estimate.
    Rate
    Labor cost is hours at the rate in the Cost header (also under Settings). Materials are dollars as entered.
    Saving
    The plan saves in this browser as you work. Export writes a JSON file you can import on another computer or share with a colleague.
