01The problem

Complex systems fail where they meet.

Sensors, platforms, software, and services each work on their own. The mission depends on the seams between them: the handoffs, interfaces, and integrations that no single program owns. That is where capability is lost, and where Astrion works.

  • Sustained value

    Applied insight that endures.

    As threats, technologies, and budgets change, the capability keeps working.

02Missions

Six mission segments. One engineering discipline.

Each segment stands on its own program record. What connects them is one discipline: prove a capability under conditions that mirror the mission before it is fielded.

  1. 01

    Integrated Air and Missile Defense

    The integration of capabilities to defend the homeland, the joint force, and allies against the full spectrum of air and missile threats, including ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, hypersonic weapons, and unmanned aircraft systems.

  2. 02

    Lifecycle Management and Cyber Warfare

    The integrated planning and execution of weapon system testing and lifecycle management. The integrated employment of offensive and defensive cyberspace operations, and cryptologic capabilities.

  3. 03

    Layered Defense, Autonomous Warfare & Integrated Fires

    The synchronized detection, identification, tracking, and defeat capabilities. Integrated employment of multi-domain autonomous systems.

    Explore the division →
  4. 04

    Space Warfighting

    The integrated employment of military spacepower to achieve and sustain space superiority: the freedom of movement and action in, from, and to space for the United States and its allies while denying the same to adversaries.

  5. 05

    Critical Infrastructure Protection

    The integrated capabilities required to identify, assess, prevent, detect, respond to, mitigate, and recover from threats and hazards to the U.S. Homeland.

  6. 06

    Exploration and Lunar Presence

    The integration of transportation, power, communications, mobility, habitation, autonomy, and in-situ resource utilization required to establish and sustain an enduring human and robotic presence on and around the Moon, creating the foundation for expansion deeper into the solar system.

05Edge

Capability that holds at the edge.

The edge is where the mission is decided: contested spectrum, disconnected networks, operators under pressure, and no time to send it back to the lab. What we field has to work there, on day one, and keep working.

  1. 01

    Built for the conditions, not the demo

    Designed and tested against the environment operators will face: degraded links, contested spectrum, weather, and time.

  2. 02

    Works disconnected

    Decision support, models, and data that keep performing when the network does not.

  3. 03

    Proven before it is fielded

    Lab, range, and field evaluation under conditions that mirror the fight, so what works in test works in the field.

  4. 04

    Sustained in place

    Monitored, updated, and kept current as threats, data, and missions change, without pulling the capability out of service.
